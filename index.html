Case study · B2B AI SaaS

DeepAgent

An AI agent studio that lets marketing, sales and support teams automate workflows and deploy AI chatbots without writing a line of code.

Role
Product Designer
Timeline
8 months
Screens
100+
Tools
Figma, FigJam
Company

The product

Two products under one roof

DeepAgent has two distinct sides, and understanding both mattered before any design work started.

AI ChatBot
A bot builder where teams train an assistant on their own data (website URLs, files, custom Q&As), style it, and embed it as a live chat or support widget. It answers repetitive questions, captures leads and hands over to a human when needed.

AI Agent Studio
A visual workflow builder for automating multi-step tasks across HubSpot, Slack, WhatsApp, Google Sheets and more. With 200+ templates for marketing, sales, support and eCommerce, teams run full workflows without technical setup.

The goal: give business teams the power of automation without relying on engineers to build it for them.

The problem

Automation built for developers

Marketers, sales reps and support teams struggle to automate their work because existing tools are either too complex (built for developers) or too limited (basic chatbots with no intelligence). They need one place to build, train and deploy AI agents visually, without code, documentation, or switching between five tools.

73%of business users have never built an agent
5+tools the average team juggles to automate one workflow
6 wksaverage wait for developer help with a workflow

Research

Competitors and the people who sell

In a 0→1 product there are no users yet, so research came from two angles. First, what people already complain about in n8n, Zapier, Tidio and Intercom, gathered from G2 and Capterra reviews, Reddit and X threads, and Trustpilot.

n8n / ZapierTidio / IntercomOur opportunity
Powerful but complex. Node-based UI overwhelms non-technical users. Steep learning curve and no built-in AI agent builder. Good for live chat but limited automation depth. No agent flow builder. Expensive for SMBs. One product that merges AI agent creation, live chat and automation, in an interface marketers, sales reps and support teams can use on their own.

Second, stakeholder interviews. The founders and sales team had been on hundreds of sales calls, so I asked them:

  • What do customers always struggle with?
  • Why do deals fall through?
  • What is the number one complaint after onboarding?

Solution 1

Train with your own data

Upload docs, FAQs, SOPs or product sheets, or point the bot at a website. The agent learns from your content instead of generic internet data, and the platform handles the prompting, so nobody needs to learn prompt engineering.

Solution 2

Launch an agent in under a minute

A single workspace for every agent, grouped by use case, filterable by status and searchable in real time. Start manually, describe the workflow to AI, or pick one of 200+ templates, then review the generated flow and publish.

Impact

What changed after launch

The biggest gains landed where they mattered most: users getting through setup, and more agents reaching live channels.

↓ 60%Drop-off during agent setup. More users finish their first flow.
3×Template adoption, from 1 in 5 users to 3 in 5 after the library was restructured
↑ 40%Active deployments per account. More agents reach live channels.
↓ 70%Support queries now solved by the chatbot

Handoff

A file developers can build from

A fully sectioned Figma file with every screen labelled, numbered and prototype-connected, from login to live agent. A developer opens it and builds.

Design task · Hospital web app

Diagnostics Lab Workflow

One loop between the doctor and the laboratory: order a test from the patient record, track the sample through the lab, and put critical results in front of a doctor first.

Role
Product Designer
Type
Design task
Platform
Web, 1440
Panels
Doctor · Laboratory

The flow

From order to reviewed result

Lab work fails in the gaps between people: a test ordered but not collected, a sample stuck in a queue, a critical value nobody saw. The design follows a sample across both panels so each handoff has an owner and a visible state.

  1. OrderThe doctor raises a lab request from the patient's record.
  2. TrackThe lab logs the sample and moves it through collection, processing and results.
  3. TriageResults are sorted by severity and waiting time, critical first.
  4. ReviewThe doctor opens the flagged values against reference ranges.

Doctor panel

Start the day with what needs you

The dashboard leads with today's patients, appointments, admissions and lab results, plus quick actions and a patient review queue with priority. From a patient record, clinical actions (lab request, radiology, admission, appointment) sit one click away.

Laboratory panel

Every sample has a state

Sample Tracking shows today's samples, STAT cases, work in progress and what needs attention, with a banner for samples at risk. Triage sorts results by severity and how long they've waited, so a critical troponin never sits behind a routine panel.

Decisions

Severity you can read at a glance

  • Severity in words and colour. Critical, High, Low and Normal always carry a label, so the meaning survives colour blindness and grayscale printouts.
  • Waiting time as a column. Triage shows how long each result has waited, which turns "urgent" into something a shift can act on.
  • STAT is a first-class priority. It is counted at the top and tagged in every row.
  • Results against reference ranges. The result page flags each value beside its range and the previous reading.

Booking starts above the fold

Mobile app · Healthcare

Appointment Booking

Booking a doctor for yourself or a family member in four steps, with the unhappy paths designed as carefully as the happy one.

Role
Product Designer
Platform
iOS, mobile
Steps
4
Screens
14

Step 1

Choose a doctor and a slot

Pick a specialty, compare doctors with fee and next availability, choose in-person or video, then a date and time. The date strip shows how many slots each day has. When a day has none, the empty state jumps straight to the next available slot instead of leaving people to tap through the week.

Step 2

Who is this for?

Most bookings are for a parent or child. Family profiles sit in one list with relationship and age, and a new member can be added inline. Date of birth is validated in place, so a mistyped year is caught before the doctor sees it.

Step 3

Health details, shared with consent

Reason for visit, known conditions, current medications and recent reports, prefilled from the family profile where possible. A clear toggle decides whether the details are shared with this doctor only.

Step 4

Review, pay, and recover

The review screen holds the slot with a visible timer while the person checks the doctor, time, patient and bill. If the slot is taken in the meantime, the app says so plainly and offers the nearest open times in one tap.
